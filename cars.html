# Car Team Year Pace Best car % Track pts Wins Podiums Poles FL Best Drivers

Car is a data-fit principal-component score of team win rate, points per race, best car rate and raw pace, placed on a rank-linear 0–100 scale across all team-seasons. Every driver who raced for the team shares that effect, and driver Car is stint-weighted. Pace (45% best grid + 55% best finish per race) and Best car % are both inputs to the Car score. Track points include teams excluded from the championship (e.g. McLaren 2007).
